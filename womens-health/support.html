Contact Support

Get help with the app, your account, or your privacy choices.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

For an emergency, contact local emergency services. The support inbox is not a clinical or emergency service.

1. Contact the Women’s Health team

Email support@wolfpack-labs.com and include WP Women's Health in the subject. You can also use More → App Settings → Contact Support inside the app.

For a technical issue, include the app version, device and operating system, what you expected, and the steps that led to the problem. Remove health details from screenshots before sharing them. Never include passwords, verification codes, or full authentication links.

Support helps with the app and account; it cannot interpret symptoms, provide treatment advice, or respond to a medical emergency.

2. Common questions

I cannot sign in or reset my password.

Use the same sign-in method you used to create the account. For an email-and-password account, request a fresh reset email from the app. See Account & security help.

How do I delete my account or request my data?

Open More → Data & privacy in the app, or use the web deletion-request instructions. You can request deletion without reinstalling the app.

Why did a reminder or forecast not appear?

Check notification permissions and reminder settings. Personal forecasts can be withheld when records are insufficient or uncertain. Neither feature is a substitute for a medical reminder or professional advice. Review Health & app disclosures.

How can I report an accessibility issue?

Tell us the screen or page and the barrier you encountered. You can report it without sharing your health history. See Accessibility.

3. Prepare a support email

Write a short message below, review the draft, then choose your email app or copy it. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself.

Use only the account email and the details needed to explain your request. Do not include health history, passwords, or codes.

Open email app

You must send the message in your email app. If no email app opens, copy the draft into your email service and send it to support@wolfpack-labs.com.
