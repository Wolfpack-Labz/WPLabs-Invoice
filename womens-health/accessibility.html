Accessibility

Ways to make the app easier to use, and a direct route to report an access barrier.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

On this page

1. Our approach2. Options in the app3. These webpages4. Report an accessibility barrier

1. Our approach

We want WP Women's Health and its supporting webpages to be usable with different reading, vision, motor, and attention needs. Accessibility is ongoing work. We aim to follow recognized accessibility practices; this statement does not claim that every feature has been independently audited or meets every accessibility standard.

2. Options in the app

Open More → App Settings for the available larger-text, high-contrast, and reduced-motion options. The app also uses operating-system accessibility settings where supported. These display choices are stored for the installation and can remain after account deletion.

Feature support depends on the device, app version, and operating system. Tell us if text is cut off, controls lack labels, keyboard or screen-reader navigation is difficult, or a chart needs a clearer explanation.

3. These webpages

These pages include a skip-to-content link, descriptive page headings and links, visible keyboard focus, native form labels, a keyboard-operable menu, and layouts that adapt to small screens. They respect your browser’s reduced-motion setting and support text zoom. Table regions can scroll horizontally when needed.

The pages work without requiring a sign-in. Direct support and deletion email links remain available without JavaScript. The optional email-draft helper needs JavaScript; you can always write directly to our support address instead.

4. Report an accessibility barrier

Email support@wolfpack-labs.com with WP Women's Health Accessibility in the subject. Describe the page or feature, your device and browser or app version, any assistive technology you choose to identify, and the kind of help you need.

You do not need to include health information to report a barrier. We review reports and work on practical alternatives or fixes. If a privacy or deletion control is inaccessible, you may make the same request by email.
