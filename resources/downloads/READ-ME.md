## PDF forms
For reliable filling and saving, download the file and open it in a PDF application that supports forms, such as Adobe Acrobat Reader. Browser and phone PDF previews vary. Save a separate completed copy and check that your entries remain visible before sending it. The PDF amount fields do not calculate automatically. The change order's approval areas are text fields, not digital signature certificates.

## Expense tracker
Open the XLSX in Excel, or import it into Google Sheets. Type in the amber cells in rows 10-209. The total includes all 200 rows even when filters hide records. Blank amounts are ignored; zero amounts count as entries; refunds can be negative. The date, vendor, category, job/purpose, amount, payment method, receipt reference and notes belong to one transaction. Use receipt filenames or private links rather than payment card information.

The free starter has 200 entry rows. Start another copy when full. If extending it yourself, extend the totals, table and validation ranges too. The CSV version contains headers and blank rows, with no formulas, formatting or dropdowns. All amounts are in USD and include any tax paid. The tracker records spending and does not determine tax deductions.
