How it works

Profit = revenue − costs. Margin (%) = profit ÷ revenue × 100. Markup (%) = profit ÷ costs × 100.

Use revenue and costs from the same sale, project or period. Results describe the costs you enter; they are not automatically gross or net margin. Losses appear as negative values. Markup is undefined when cost is zero.
