How it works

Total cost = labor hours × labor cost per hour + materials + other job costs + allocated overhead. Price = total cost ÷ (1 − target margin ÷ 100).

The result is before sales tax and reflects only the costs you enter. A 20% margin means 20% of the selling price remains after those costs.
