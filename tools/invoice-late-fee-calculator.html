How it works

Monthly: balance × rate ÷ 100 × days ÷ 30. Annual: balance × rate ÷ 100 × days ÷ 365. Daily: balance × rate ÷ 100 × days. One-time: balance × rate ÷ 100 when days late is greater than zero.

These methods use simple fees with no compounding. The monthly and annual methods use fixed 30-day and 365-day bases. Use a rate and method allowed by your agreement and applicable rules. This tool calculates amounts and does not apply charges.
