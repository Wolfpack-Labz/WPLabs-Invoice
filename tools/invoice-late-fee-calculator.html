How it works

Monthly fees use balance × rate × days ÷ 30. Daily fees use balance × rate × days. One-time fees use balance × rate when days late is greater than zero. These are simple calculations without compounding. This estimates arithmetic; use only a rate and method permitted by your agreement and applicable rules.
