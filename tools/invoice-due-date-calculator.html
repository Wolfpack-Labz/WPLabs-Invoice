How it works

Due date = invoice date + the selected number of calendar days. The invoice date is day zero. For example, January 1 with Net 30 is January 31.

Calendar days include weekends. Moving a weekend date to Monday is optional and does not account for holidays. Follow your agreed payment terms; the calculator does not change them.
