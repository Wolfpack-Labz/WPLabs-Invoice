Website Accessibility

Help using the website’s content, navigation, and interactive tools.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Applies to general website use. Find the separate app policies →
On this page1. Our approach2. Website features and controls3. Report a barrier4. App accessibility

1. Our approach

We aim to make the website usable with different devices, reading needs, keyboard navigation, and assistive technology. Accessibility work is ongoing. We do not claim that every page or downloadable file has been independently certified against an accessibility standard.

2. Website features and controls

The website includes skip links, headings, visible keyboard focus, labeled controls, a mobile menu, and layouts that adapt to different widths. Motion settings are respected where supported, and browsers can enlarge text. Some tables and resource formats may require scrolling or a compatible viewer.

Calculator results and editable worksheets rely on JavaScript. If a control or file is difficult to use, contact us for help with the content or a practical alternative.

3. Report a barrier

Email support@wolfpack-labs.com with the page URL, what you were trying to do, the barrier, and your browser or assistive technology if you choose to share it. Avoid including private account or health information in screenshots.

You can make a privacy request by email if a form or page is inaccessible. See Website Data Requests.

4. App accessibility

This statement concerns wolfpack-labs.com. The Women’s Health app has a separate Accessibility page. For Invoice app accessibility help, contact its support channel.
