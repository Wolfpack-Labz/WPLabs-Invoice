Cookies & Browser Data

A practical explanation of browser data on the general website.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Applies to general website use. Find the separate app policies →
On this page1. Current general website behavior2. Page state, URLs, and downloads3. Hosting and linked services4. Your controls5. Questions

1. Current general website behavior

The general website does not currently include advertising pixels or third-party behavioral analytics scripts. Its normal public pages do not require a website account or a website login cookie. Optional services linked from the website can have their own account and cookie behavior.

This notice covers the general website. App authentication, customer payment pages, and external providers should also be read with their applicable product or provider policies.

2. Page state, URLs, and downloads

Carousel controls, calculator results, and editable worksheets use state in the current page. Blog search, filters, and page choices are reflected in the URL so browser navigation can restore the view. That URL can remain in browser history and appear in ordinary server request records.

Downloaded files, printed documents, browser caches, and saved copies are managed by your browser, device, or chosen storage service. Clearing a page’s state or closing the browser does not delete an email you sent to support.

3. Hosting and linked services

Website hosting, security infrastructure, and the contact service process technical request information to provide and protect the service. When you open Apple, Google, Stripe, or another third-party site, its own settings and policies apply. The Website Privacy Policy describes website providers and contact information.

4. Your controls

You can use your browser settings to clear history and cached data, restrict cookies, or disable JavaScript. Restrictions can limit interactive calculators, search controls, forms, or menus. Direct email links remain an alternative for website contact and privacy requests.

The website does not currently change its behavior for Do Not Track signals. Our no-sale/no-cross-context-advertising-sharing practice applies independently of that signal. We will address applicable notice and permission requirements before introducing relevant new tracking features.

5. Questions

Contact support@wolfpack-labs.com for questions about website browser data or to make a privacy request.
