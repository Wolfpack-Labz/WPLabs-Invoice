Website Data Requests

Make a request about a website message or other website personal information.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Applies to general website use. Find the separate app policies →
On this page1. Send a website privacy request2. Website information and app records3. Verification and timing4. Retention and review

1. Send a website privacy request

Email support@wolfpack-labs.com with the subject Wolfpack Labs Website Privacy Request. State whether you want access, correction, a copy, deletion, withdrawal of applicable consent, or review of an earlier decision.

Include the email address used in your website correspondence and enough context to locate the request, such as an approximate message date. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, card numbers, government IDs, or detailed health records in the initial email.

Email a website privacy request

You send the email yourself; opening this page or clicking an email link does not submit a request.

2. Website information and app records

A website request can concern contact-form messages, direct correspondence, or other identifiable website information. It does not automatically delete an app account or app records.

For product records, use WP Invoice Data Requests & Deletion or Women’s Health Data Requests & Deletion. Invoice recipients can also read the Customer / Invoice Recipient Notice.

3. Verification and timing

We take proportionate steps to verify a request before releasing or deleting personal information. Sending from the same email address can help, but we can discuss reasonable alternatives. We respond within applicable legal deadlines and explain a permitted extension or refusal when required.

4. Retention and review

Deletion can be limited by a specific legal obligation or another lawful basis, including necessary security or request-handling records. Restricted backup copies may take additional time under applicable requirements. We can explain any relevant exception for your request.

If you disagree with our decision, reply with Website Privacy Request Appeal and the reason for your concern. Rights and complaint options depend on applicable law. See the Website Privacy Policy.
