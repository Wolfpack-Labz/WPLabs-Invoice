## Upload the article-only package
Upload blog/ and _drafts/ from this package into the root of your current GitHub Pages repository and commit the changes. This assumes you already installed the automatic-blog update plus the Free Tools and Downloads update. It adds six articles and changes the draft template's next Field Notes number to 20.

Do not edit index.html or blog.html to add cards. Their existing Jekyll loops discover the new metadata and rebuild the homepage, Blog listing, category filters and blog sitemap entries. The newest five are Field Notes 19, 18, 17, 16 and 15. Field Note 14 remains on the full Blog page.
